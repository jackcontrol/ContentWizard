## What is the Difference Between a Remaster and a Re-Mix?

A remaster is a new master recording created from the original master tapes or files, while a re-mix is a new mix of the original tracks. If you're looking to update the sound of your old releases, we recommend a remaster, as it allows us to optimize the sound for vinyl playback while preserving the original mix.
