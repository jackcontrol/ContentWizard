## Mastering for Bandcamp vs Streaming: Format Strategy Basics
For DIY heavy bands, mastering is a crucial step in preparing your music for online distribution. The primary goal is to create a consistent, high-quality sound that translates well across various formats and platforms.

### Bandcamp vs Streaming: Key Format Differences
- **Bandcamp**: High-resolution audio (16-bit/44.1 kHz or 24-bit/96 kHz) is recommended for optimal playback on the platform.
- **Streaming services (e.g., Spotify, Apple Music)**: They typically use 16-bit/44.1 kHz MP3 or AAC for streaming, and 24-bit/44.1 kHz or 96 kHz for premium or high-fidelity playback.

### Mastering for Bandcamp: Focus on High-Resolution Audio
To prepare your music for Bandcamp, it's essential to focus on mastering for high-resolution audio formats. This will ensure your music sounds its best when uploaded to the platform.

- **Bit depth**: Master at 24-bit minimum for optimal sound quality.
- **Sample rate**: Aim for 96 kHz or higher for the best possible playback.
- **Format**: Deliver a 24-bit/96 kHz WAV or AIFF file.

### Mastering for Streaming: Focus on Format Compatibility
For streaming services, you'll want to focus on mastering for the most common formats used by these platforms.

- **Bit depth**: 16-bit is acceptable for most streaming services, but 24-bit is recommended for higher-quality playback.
- **Sample rate**: 44.1 kHz is standard for most streaming services, but 96 kHz is also supported by some platforms.
- **Format**: Deliver a 16-bit/44.1 kHz or 24-bit/96 kHz MP3, AAC, or WAV file.

### Using Multiple Formats: A Practical Approach
To simplify your mastering process, consider using a single master that can be easily converted to various formats for different platforms.

- **Single master**: Create a single 24-bit/96 kHz master that can be downsampled to 16-bit/44.1 kHz for streaming services.
- **Format conversion**: Use software to convert your master to the required formats for each platform.
